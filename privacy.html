Privacy Policy – Clip Factory

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Overview

Clip Factory is a private desktop tool used only by its operator to upload their own video clips to their own TikTok account. It does not collect personal data from other people.

2. Data processed

  • TikTok access and refresh tokens of the operator's own account, obtained through TikTok Login Kit after the operator grants permission.
  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) of the operator's account, used only to confirm the connection.
  • The video files and captions the operator chooses to upload.

3. Storage

Tokens are stored only locally on the operator's computer. No data is sent to any server other than TikTok's official API. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens can be deleted at any time by removing the local token file or by revoking the app's access in the TikTok app settings.

5. Third parties

Data is transmitted only to TikTok in order to upload content. TikTok's own privacy policy applies to that processing.
